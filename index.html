The Atelier

A small studio with an unreasonable standard.

KM BLOOMS began the way most good things do — with one bouquet, made properly, for someone who mattered. What started as a favour became a practice: sourcing the best stems available each week, conditioning them slowly, and building arrangements that are composed rather than assembled.

We keep the studio deliberately small. Every order passes through the same pair of hands, and every conversation happens directly with us — usually on WhatsApp, often at short notice, always with care.

Important Information

Ordering, payment and delivery — clearly set out.

Read the policies Pricing & packages
  • Orders are confirmed only after a 50% non-refundable booking fee has been paid.
  • Orders must be placed at least 3 days before the required delivery or collection date.
  • Same-day orders are subject to flower availability.
  • Clients must provide their full name, contact number and delivery or collection details when placing an order.
  • A 50% booking fee is required to confirm your order.
  • The remaining 50% balance must be paid in full before delivery or collection.
  • Full payment is required for orders placed less than 24 hours before delivery or collection.
  • No bouquet will be prepared or dispatched until payment has been received and confirmed.
  • The booking fee is non-refundable.
  • Delivery is charged separately and is not included in bouquet prices.
  • Delivery fees vary depending on your location.
  • Clients are welcome to collect their orders at the agreed collection point.
  • Delivery times will be confirmed once payment has been received.
  • Fresh flowers are subject to seasonal availability.
  • If a specific flower or colour is unavailable, we will contact you before making any substitutions.
  • Every bouquet is handcrafted, therefore slight variations in arrangement may occur.
  • We strive to match every design as closely as possible to our photos.
  • Fresh flowers are natural products. While we take every care to ensure quality, slight variations in bloom size, colour and appearance may occur.
